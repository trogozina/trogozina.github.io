About

I am an architectural designer based in Hamburg, with four years of practice in Moscow, an internship at gmp in Berlin and a current role in BIM coordination at DESY. My work ranges from housing, schools and kindergartens to urban masterplans.

My professional foundation was built over four years at Atrium, an award-winning architectural practice in Moscow, where I was promoted to Lead Architect. I led teams of around five architects, coordinated with engineering consultants and took projects from concept design to permit planning. After moving to Germany, I spent six months at gmp Architekten in Berlin, working on the masterplan and design guidelines for Academic City Yerevan and on projects in China.

Alongside completing my Master’s degree at Hochschule Wismar, I work as a BIM coordinator (student assistant) at DESY (Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron) in Hamburg, helping to set up client-side BIM processes for a large programme of new campus buildings.

Through my studies and practice in Germany, I have gained a working knowledge of the HOAI service phases and the German BIM guidelines (VDI 2552). My Master’s thesis on Refshaleøen in Copenhagen explores the adaptive reuse of industrial heritage and sustainable urban development.

I work mainly in Revit and Rhino, alongside AutoCAD and Adobe Creative Cloud, and I am particularly interested in energy-efficient design. I work fluently in English and Russian and am improving my intermediate German.
