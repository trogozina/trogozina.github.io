About

I am an architectural designer and BIM coordinator based in Hamburg, with over four years of professional experience across a diverse range of typologies—from housing and educational facilities to complex masterplans and research infrastructure. I approach architecture by balancing strong aesthetic vision with rigorous technical execution and digital process management.

My professional foundation was built during 4.5 years at Atrium, an award-winning architectural practice in Moscow. There, I advanced to a team lead role, managing small teams, coordinating with engineers, and overseeing projects through all phases, from initial concept to construction documentation. Following my move to Germany, I spent six months at gmp Architekten in Berlin, contributing to large-scale masterplans and design guidelines for international developments in Armenia and China.

Currently, alongside concluding my Master’s degree at Hochschule Wismar, I am working with DESY (Deutsches Elektronen-Synchrotron) in Hamburg. In this role, I am helping to spearhead the implementation of BIM into their digital workflows, establishing new processes from the ground up in close collaboration with project stakeholders.

Through my academic and practical experience in Germany, I have developed a solid understanding of the HOAI framework and German BIM standards. While my portfolio demonstrates versatility across various sectors, my academic research—including my Master’s thesis on Copenhagen’s Refshaleøen—explores sustainable urban development, adaptive reuse, and integrating high-density residential planning into complex sites.

I am proficient in Revit, Rhino, AutoCAD and Adobe Creative Suite and hold a strong interest in energy-efficient design. I work fluently in English and Russian, and I am actively advancing my German (currently A2).
